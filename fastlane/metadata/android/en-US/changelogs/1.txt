* First release of Easy Phone
* Dialer and contacts manager in one app
* Call history opens with an inline dialpad and one call button per SIM
* Typing on the dialpad filters calls and contacts instantly
* WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business shortcuts, with a country picker for local numbers
* Blocked calls stay in the history in red, blocked numbers reachable from the menu
