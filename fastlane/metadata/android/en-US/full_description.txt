Easy Phone puts your dialer and your contacts in one simple, private app.

<b>📞 A complete phone app</b>
• Fast dialpad with contact search and speed dial
• Call history grouped by contact, with SIM information
• Favorites for the people you call most
• Clean in-call screen with conference calls, hold, mute and speaker
• Call blocking, including hidden numbers and numbers not in your contacts

<b>👥 A full contacts manager</b>
• View and edit contact details with all phone numbers, emails, addresses and events
• Create groups and manage contacts in bulk
• Import and export contacts as vCard files, with automatic scheduled backups
• Merge duplicate contacts and choose which contact sources to show

<b>💬 WhatsApp shortcuts</b>
• Open a WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business chat with any number in one tap
• Available in the call history, on the dialpad and inside contact details
• No need to save the number first

<b>🔒 Private by design</b>
• No ads, no tracking, no accounts
• Your contacts and call history never leave your phone
• Open source under the GPL-3.0 license

<b>🎨 Make it yours</b>
• Light, dark and custom colors
• Choose which tabs to show and how contacts are sorted

Easy Phone is based on the open-source Fossify Phone and Fossify Contacts apps.
